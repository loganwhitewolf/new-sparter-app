## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-76-04 | Information Disclosure | getReimbursementList | high | mitigate | The query's WHERE clause scopes exclusively on `r.user_id = userId` (from the verified session, never a request param) — a cross-user row can never appear, mirroring getReimbursementAggregates's existing IDOR-safe-by-construction convention. |
| T-76-05 | Elevation of Privilege | getReimbursementList | medium | mitigate | `expense_id IS NOT NULL` in the WHERE clause is a hard DAL-level filter, not just a UI omission — a Group-anchored reimbursement (RMB-08 descoped, dormant) can never be returned even if one existed for the user, closing the "Group-anchor surfaced via a new surface" risk at its source. |
</threat_model>
