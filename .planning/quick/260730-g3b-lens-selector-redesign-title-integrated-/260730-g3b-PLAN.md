Replace the Phase 80 pill segmented `LensSwitch` with a title-integrated dropdown per the locked
`.planning/lens-selector-DECISIONS.md` (LSD-01..LSD-05): the lens moves into the page heading as a
text trigger with two described `menuitemradio` options, the control disappears entirely for users
with no amortization plans and on the lens-invariant `/dashboard/tags` surface, and the overview
monthly chart gains a dashed cash-lens overlay line visible only while `competenza` is active.

switches, unglossed jargon, noise for plan-less users, a dead disabled control on `/tags`, and
custom styling outside the design system) without touching lens semantics — `?lens=` stays
canonical, `lens-persistence.ts` is untouched, and `resolveLedgerRowSource()` remains the only
cash/accrual seam.

`hasAmortizationPlans` DAL gate wired into the three surfaces that show the control, `/dashboard/tags`
with no lens control at all, and a conditional dashed "Uscite (cassa)" overlay line on the overview
chart, competenza-only.
</objective>
