<objective>
Migrate `lib/dal/overview.ts`'s two remaining netting-fragment aggregation sites
(getMonthOverMonthCategoryChanges — movers drill-down; getOverviewChart — the 12-month bar chart)
to the `ledgerRowSource` pattern proven in Plan 80-01. Separately — and architecturally distinct —
make the two NAVIGATION functions (`getYearsWithData`, `getMonthsWithData`) lens-aware per D-09:
these are NOT netting-fragment aggregation sites, so they must NOT take a `ledgerRowSource` object;
their cash branch stays completely untouched raw SQL (byte-identical, LENS-03), and only the
accrual branch adds a UNION against `amortization_instalment`. Extend `resolveYear`'s fallback
contract with the D-10 cross-lens clamp.

Purpose: LENS-04 (whole selected year including future instalment months) and LENS-05 (the year
selector can offer an instalment-only year) both hinge on these two navigation functions plus the
cross-lens clamp — this is where "an instalment can create a year with no transaction in it"
(ADR 0019 §6) gets handled correctly instead of silently hiding a year the dashboard could render.

Output: getOverviewChart/getMonthOverMonthCategoryChanges are lens-selectable; getYearsWithData/
getMonthsWithData are lens-aware; resolveYear clamps cross-lens period mismatches.
</objective>
