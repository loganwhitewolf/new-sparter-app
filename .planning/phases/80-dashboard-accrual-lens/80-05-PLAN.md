<objective>
Wire the two Categories routes (`/dashboard/categories`, `/dashboard/categories/[id]`) to the lens:
parse `?lens=`, render the shared `LensSwitch` (built in Plan 80-01), and thread the resolved
`ledgerRowSource` into `getCategoryRanking`/`getCategoryDeviations` (list page) and
`getCategoryDetail`/`getCategoryDeviations` (detail page) — both migrated to accept it in Plan
80-02.

These two routes have no year selector (they use the preset-based `DashboardFilters` component,
distinct from `/dashboard/overview`'s year `Select`) — the domain_grounding's "next to the year
selector" placement guidance does not apply literally here; place `LensSwitch` near each page's own
title/filters row instead, since D-03 only requires the switch be present and behave identically,
not that it sit beside a control that does not exist on this route.

Purpose: LENS-01 (global switch on every dashboard sub-route) and LENS-02 (category breakdown/
ranking/deviations/detail reflect the lens) become user-visible on these two routes.
Output: category ranking, deviations, and detail all update with the lens; the switch is visible
and functional on both routes.
</objective>
